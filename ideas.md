# Design Direction — A Little Place That’s Only Ours

## Three Possible Directions

### Theme Name: Paper Moon Postcards
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile, editorial love-letter site inspired by handwritten correspondence, old postcards, and the quiet glow of a desk lamp. It makes distance feel intimate rather than tragic.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Soft Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, contemporary interface built around little digital signals—typing dots, timestamps, voice-note marks, and shared skies. Tender, minimal, and quietly optimistic.
**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Afterglow Cinema
**Very Brief Intro:** A cinematic, dusk-toned experience with film-strip moments, slow reveals, and a sense of looking back through a warm memory. More dramatic and atmospheric than scrapbook-like.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Paper Moon Postcards

### Design Movement
Contemporary romantic editorial design with references to analog ephemera, Japanese stationery, and quiet luxury print layouts.

### Core Principles
1. Make the page feel touched by hand: paper texture, postage marks, imperfect lines, and handwritten accents.
2. Use asymmetry and generous negative space so the content feels discovered, not stacked.
3. Let distance become a visual motif through constellations, coordinates, and a gentle “same moon” thread.
4. Keep the emotional tone sincere and specific, never overly sugary or generic.

### Color Philosophy
The base is warm parchment rather than white, creating the feeling of a letter opened at night. Ink-black text gives the site literary weight; rust-red and faded coral act like stamps and pressed petals; a deep midnight blue anchors the sections that talk about distance. The signature color is **postmark vermilion** — a restrained red-orange that feels human, tactile, and ownable.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial scroll with alternating left-anchored and right-anchored blocks, narrow reading columns beside wide atmospheric fields, and occasional “postcard” cards that interrupt the page like found objects. The page should not read as a centered marketing landing page.

### Signature Elements
1. A small circular postmark emblem with a heart-and-orbit mark.
2. Fine red thread lines that connect section labels, dates, and memory cards.
3. Handwritten-style marginal notes and tiny coordinate/timestamp captions.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like unfolding a letter: buttons press with a slight paper shift, memory cards lift softly, and the final “open this when you miss me” moment reveals itself as a deliberate keepsake. Nothing should flash or compete with the emotional content.

### Animation
Use gentle fade-and-rise entrances with 30–70ms staggered timing, a slow orbit for the heart emblem, and a subtle paper-lift on hover. Keep all transitions under 300ms except the rare final reveal, which can breathe up to 500ms. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Display: **Cormorant Garamond** in semibold/italic for intimate headlines and pull quotes. Body: **DM Sans** for readable paragraphs and utility text. Accent: **Caveat** for marginal notes, signatures, and handwritten microcopy. Use uppercase mono-like tracking for dates and labels.

### Brand Essence
A private digital love letter for two people making a home across the miles — intimate, thoughtful, quietly cinematic. Personality adjectives: **tender, observant, devoted**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are warm and specific. CTAs sound like invitations rather than instructions. Microcopy uses small sensory details and honest affection.

Example lines:
- “For the girl who makes the distance feel worth crossing.”
- “Press play on the little things I wish I could hand you in person.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A circular postmark-like emblem containing a small heart orbiting a four-point star, paired with a lowercase wordmark set in Cormorant Garamond with a hand-drawn underline. The symbol must work without text as a favicon/avatar.

### Signature Brand Color
**Postmark Vermilion — #C95745**, used sparingly for thread lines, stamps, small highlights, and emotional punctuation.

## Style Decisions
- No generic centered hero; the opening uses a split editorial composition.
- No purple gradients, excessive rounded cards, or default UI styling.
- Keep the site personal through placeholders that are easy to customize: names, monthsary date, shared memory labels, and the final sign-off.
